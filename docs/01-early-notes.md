## 8. Revision Notes (v1.1)

1. **Notation harmonized (Gemini #1).** Added Convention M0.2: an internal-language/subobject reading (`U ↣ X`, `⊑` as mono factorization, `∈` as internal proposition) under which categorical and lattice notations are two renderings of one language. μ3, ν3, μ4, ν4 restated purely in subobject terms.
2. **Distributive law signature cleaned (Gemini #2).** Removed the trivial `F∘Id ⇒ Id∘F`; B2 now states `λ : F∘T ⇒ T∘F` with explicit unit and multiplication laws, plus the dual comonad law. B1's "close to identity" replaced by an exact iff-equation (mutually inverse ⟺ fixed point of F).
3. **Carrier pullback specified (Gemini #3).** `×_X` in I11 is now defined as a pullback in M over the common carrier, with both legs named and an explicit failure condition.
4. **Informal side conditions replaced (Gemini #4).** "Negative-safe in Y" → covariant bifunctor + ω-cocontinuity in Y (I2); "uniform in both variables" → Dubuc–Street dinaturality (I5).
5. **Terminology corrected beyond the review.** Pre-/post-fixed point labels were inverted against Knaster–Tarski: μ = least **pre**-fixed (μ4, μ5, Bd2), ν = greatest **post**-fixed (ν4, ν5, Bd3). All six entries now read `F(U) ⊑ U ⟹ μF ⊑ U` and `U ⊑ F(U) ⟹ U ⊑ νF` consistently.

## 9. Open Questions & Exploration Findings (v1.2)

Seven questions the framework raises but does not settle, each explored against the categorical and coalgebraic literature. Findings from that exploration are folded back into the axioms where they demand amendment.

| # | Open question | Finding from the literature | Status in Mordor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OQ1** | Is dinaturality (I5's side condition) actually sufficient, given that dinatural transformations notoriously fail to compose? | Dinaturals are not closed under composition except under special conditions (acyclic argument connections, pullback/pushout squares); the composability problem is studied by Dubuc–Street, McCusker–Santamaria (CSL 2018), and Petrić (g-dinaturality with argument graphs). Moreover, dinaturality does not always characterize the least-fixed-point operator (Fiore–Plotkin 1994: mild sufficient conditions, but failure in general). | **Resolved (v1.3).** I5 now carries the exact McCusker–Santamaria criterion: a composite of dinaturals is dinatural precisely when the composite argument graph is acyclic ("no cyclic connections"). A Godement-style calculus for dinaturals exists under the same graph discipline (Santamaria). |
| **OQ2** | Does D2 (final dialgebras) hold without extra assumptions? | No. Final objects are *frequently absent* in categories of dialgebras — dialgebraic semantics is often delivered by quotients instead. Initial dialgebras behave much better: they exist for inductive-inductive types (Altenkirch, Morris, Forsberg, Setzer, CALCO 2011), dialgebras going back to Lambek's "subequalizers" and Hagino. | **Amended.** D2 now carries an explicit existence assumption; D1/D3 stand as-is. Remaining open: exact conditions on (F, G) — polynomial functors on a complete M are the safe class. |
| **OQ3** | When is "balancedness" (μF ≅ νF, Bd1 equality, B1) achievable? | Freyd's *algebraic compactness* demands μ ≅ ν for **every** endofunctor, and very few categories satisfy it — Freyd's own remarks note how restrictive the notion is, and the literature records how scarce genuine examples are. | **Localized, not global.** Bd1 treats balance per-functor, which survives; wholesale compactness would force a near-degenerate M. Partial (v1.3): the canonical morphism μF → νF is not structureless — the theory of well-founded coalgebras identifies μF with the well-founded part of νF, giving Bd1 a canonical witness whose image is exactly the well-founded behavior. Characterizing balanced functors inside Set-like M remains open. |
| **OQ4** | How long are the approximation chains really (Bd4, Bd6)? | Sharper than stated: Worrell proved the final chain of a **finitary** Set functor stabilizes in ω + ω steps (strictly beyond ω), with the ω-th stage consisting of (modally) saturated trees; the full powerset functor's final chain runs through all ordinals via i-saturated trees. Conversely, Adámek et al. showed initial algebras can exist **without any iteration assumption** (via Hartogs' lemma). | **Amended.** Bd4 now cites the ω + ω bound. M0 could in principle be weakened for the μ-side. Open: exact ordinal bounds for M beyond Set. |
| **OQ5** | Is I7's original claim — up-to soundness as a pure theorem of λ-bialgebra structure — exactly right? | No: the Pous–Sangiorgi theory shows up-to techniques are sound only under **compatibility/respectfulness** conditions (progression lifting); companion-based refinements and newer sufficient conditions (e.g., bisimulation up-to elaboration) strictly subsume the classical ones. | **Amended.** I7 now requires compatibility as a side condition. Open: does λ-bialgebra structure imply compatibility for the context-up-to function in general? (Known for GSOS-like laws; open beyond.) |
| **OQ6** | Could the interaction rules I1–I5 be strengthened to unconditional μ/ν commutation? | No: Bradfield's theorem that the modal μ-calculus **alternation hierarchy is strict** means no finite commutation scheme collapses μ/ν alternations on Set-like semantics. Strictness is the meta-bound on Mordor's interaction calculus. | **Confirmed as a limit.** I1's always-direction and I2/I5's conditional directions are the best possible shape. Partial (v1.3): a precise candidate class emerges from the Bekić decomposition — for pairwise-commuting monotone one-step maps, simultaneous and nested fixed points agree (Bekić's lemma). I5 plausibly holds exactly where the μ- and ν-one-step maps commute pairwise; verifying this equivalence is the remaining open problem. |
| **OQ7** | Does the distributive law λ of B2 always exist — and when invertible, is I11's pullback well-founded? | Distributive laws of monad over monad frequently fail to exist (iterated covariant powerset is not a monad); *monotone weak* distributive laws rescue several such cases (Goy–Petrisan). Distributive laws of a monad over a plain endofunctor correspond to liftings to Eilenberg–Moore categories (Barr; Lenisa–Power–Watanabe), which is the correct existence question for B2. | **Resolved as a reformulation (v1.3).** The correct interface between the μ-layer monad and ν-layer comonad is a *monad–comonad interaction law* (McDermott–Rivas–Uustalu): with T = F* (free monad) and D = F† (cofree comonad), interaction laws exist as monoids and admit residual variants — B2/I11 should be stated over this interface. Fallback when no law exists: Garner's criterion — monotone weak laws exist just when the relevant structure is weakly cartesian (preserves weak pullbacks); in lifted-powerset settings existence reduces to preservation of decomposable morphisms (Goy–Petrisan). |

**Corroborations found along the way:**

- Theorem 3's example is not merely tight — it is canonical: ℕ ∪ {∞} arises as the final dialgebra `ν(Id, 1+Id)` in the fibrational-dialgebra literature.
- B2's shape (law of a monad over an endofunctor, `F∘T ⇒ T∘F`, EM-lifting semantics) is standard and correctly stated since v1.1.
- v1.1's pre-/post-fixed terminology fix matches the standard convention (lattice-theoretic fixed-point terminology per Knaster–Tarski).
- I4's guardedness rule has an exact literature anchor: the guarded solution theorem for iteratable functors (unique solutions of guarded equation morphisms).
