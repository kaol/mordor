## 10. Second Exploration Round (v1.3)

The four questions left pending in §9 were probed further:

| # | Question carried from §9 | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **OQ1** | Exact composability condition for dinaturals in I5 | **Resolved.** Acyclicity of the composite argument graph is necessary and sufficient for a composite of dinaturals to remain dinatural; a Godement calculus for dinaturals exists under the same graph discipline. I5 amended. |
| **OQ3** | Structure of the canonical morphism μF → νF | **Advanced.** Well-founded coalgebra theory identifies μF with the well-founded part of νF: the sandwich map of Bd1 is canonical, with image exactly the well-founded behavior. Full characterization of balanced functors stays open. |
| **OQ6** | Exact functor class where μX.νY ≅ νY.μX | **Advanced.** Bekić's lemma for pairwise-commuting monotone maps gives a precise candidate: I5 should hold exactly where the μ- and ν-one-step maps commute pairwise. Equivalence unverified. |
| **OQ7** | Existence of λ between the free monad and cofree comonad | **Resolved by reformulation.** Monad–comonad interaction laws are the native structure (laws form monoids; residual laws cover non-interacting pairs). Weak-law fallbacks are characterized: weak cartesianness (Garner), decomposable-morphism preservation (Goy–Petrisan). |

**Remaining open problems, ranked by stakes:**

1. **The Bekić–I5 conjecture** (from OQ6): μX.νY.F ≅ νY.μX.F iff the μ- and ν-one-step maps of F commute pairwise. If true, Mordor's interaction calculus has a complete commutation criterion.
2. **Balanced functor characterization** (from OQ3): which F on Set-like M have μF ↣ νF an isomorphism? Well-foundedness of all νF-behavior is the conjectural answer.
3. **Dialgebraic Lambek failure zone** (from OQ2): for which (F, G) is the initial dialgebra δ₀ : F(μFG) → G(μFG) "as iso as possible", and what replaces Lambek's lemma outside that zone?
4. **Compatibility from interaction** (from OQ5): does every monad–comonad interaction law induce a compatible up-to function on νF, making I7's compatibility side condition automatic? **Resolved — yes, in the fibrational setting** (see §11).
